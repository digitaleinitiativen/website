Vorarlberg’s Sizzlin Summer Bash is the flagship gathering of Vorarlberg’s forward thinkers, provincial urbanists and doers. We’re a lose yet connected brunch of people, organisations and initiatives based in Vorarlberg, who are making things happen together.

![](/media/image-19-1024x682.png)

### **We’re brining Sizzlin back** and you’re invited.

**TL’DR: June 3, 2026 starting at 18PM in the data:room in Hard.**

[→ Get your ticket here](https://www.eventbrite.de/e/sizzlin-summer-bash-2026-tickets-1987807548889?aff=oddtdtcreator)

Join us on June 3, 2026, at the data:room in Hard for an evening of good conversations, relaxed vibes, and a bit of playful competition. Doors open at 7:00 PM, and as always, there will be space to connect, exchange ideas, and just enjoy the moment together.

The venue also offers a few game options, so expect a mix of hanging out and lighthearted fun.Just like last year, the evening brings together a range of independent communities from across Vorarlberg — people who create spaces, organize events, and keep ideas flowing outside of traditional structures. Expect a familiar mix of perspectives, new faces, and returning ones, all contributing to a shared, open atmosphere.

Before the main event, we’ll kick things off with something special:  
At 6:00 PM, we’ll record a live podcast episode exploring the question:  
**“Why independent communities are important for Vorarlberg?”**

With voices from different communities in the room, this will be an open and honest conversation about what drives engagement, collaboration, and grassroots initiatives in the region. Whether you’re part of a community, curious about building one, or just interested in the topic, you’re very welcome to join the recording.Come by for the conversation, stay for the atmosphere.

We’re looking forward to seeing you there.
