English Version of Scrum Club Dornbirn, Digital Meetup on May 13th at 18:30 CET via Zoom.

Have you ever wondered how teams can work together collaboratively at scale? Cesario Ramos is one of the leading international experts in the field of Large Scaled Scrum. We are happy to announce that Cesario Ramos will be live in the digital version of the Scrum Club Dornbirn.

Join the Meetup on May 13th at 18:30 CET via Zoom:  
**[https://us02web.zoom.us/j/88221560743](https://us02web.zoom.us/j/88221560743)**  
(We’ll help to check your equipment from 17:30 to 18:30 on the same channel)

**Agenda**  
17:30 Soundcheck (optional)  
18:30 Official Start with Check-In  
19:00 Impulse Lecture with Cesario Ramos  
19:20 Workshop in small groups  
20:00 Presentation of the findings from the small groups  
20:20 Feedback round  
20:45 Closing  
21:00 Thank you & End
